# Sensor Data Dictionary

TrailSense has accumulated approximately 2.8 million user activity sessions since 2023. The highest-quality fields are route geometry, pace, cadence proxy, elevation gain, recovery stops, weather context, battery temperature, and device fit warnings.

Data is partitioned by anonymized account id, device model, firmware version, coarse region, sport type, and session timestamp. About 64 percent of records have enough route and elevation coverage to support personalized endurance trend analysis.

Important positive signal: the company can compute non-medical readiness and training load indicators from repeated activity patterns, recovery pauses, route difficulty, and weather exposure. This supports a subscription analytics SaaS for outdoor coaching teams, race organizers, and premium consumers.

Hard limitation: TrailSense does not have labeled clinical outcomes, physician-reviewed diagnoses, ECG-grade waveforms, or medical consent. The data dictionary explicitly warns that the dataset cannot validate disease detection, arrhythmia diagnosis, heat-stroke diagnosis, or any medical treatment recommendation.
