# Partnership Pipeline

Potential partners include mountain guide associations, endurance coaching networks, outdoor race organizers, and insurance wellness programs. The strongest pilot conversations are with guide operators that manage groups and need post-trip reports.

Two partners asked for a "weekly route readiness and training trend briefing" that can be exported as PDF for coaches and customers.

No hospital, clinical research organization, or medical device partner is currently committed. The business development team labels medical diagnostics as a future exploration, not a 2026 launch candidate.
