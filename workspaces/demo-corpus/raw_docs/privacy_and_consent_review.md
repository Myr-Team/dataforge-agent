# Privacy And Consent Review

Current consent allows fitness analytics, route summaries, product improvement, and anonymized aggregate benchmarking. It does not allow clinical diagnosis, sale of identifiable health predictions, or medical decision support.

A compliant data product can use anonymized cohort benchmarks, personal trend summaries, event reporting, and opt-in coaching exports. It should expose deletion and export controls in the customer workspace.

Hard blocker for health diagnosis products: the legal review requires explicit medical consent, clinical governance, validated outcome labels, and a process for adverse-event handling. None of those exist today.

The review recommends confidence labels for all generated claims: data-confirmed, market-inferred, and speculative.
