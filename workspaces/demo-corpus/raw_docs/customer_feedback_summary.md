# Customer Feedback Summary Q1-Q2

Customers consistently ask for three capabilities: better trip risk summaries, longitudinal training insights, and group leader dashboards for guided hikes. Power users want to compare effort across routes with different elevation and weather conditions.

Subscription willingness appears strongest among outdoor clubs, coaching studios, mountain guide operators, and event organizers. They already export CSV files and build manual spreadsheets after events.

Negative signal: consumer users reject scary health language. Interviews show that "diagnosis", "medical risk", and "disease prediction" wording reduces trust. Users prefer phrasing such as "training trend", "route strain", "recovery suggestion", and "safety preparation".

Support tickets also show a data quality gap: about 18 percent of sessions have interrupted GPS in canyons or dense forests. Any product must show confidence and explain missing segments.
