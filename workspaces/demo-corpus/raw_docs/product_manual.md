# TrailSense RidgeBand Product Manual

TrailSense RidgeBand is an outdoor activity band for trail running, hiking, cycling, and guided trekking groups. The current hardware line includes heart-rate sampling, barometric altitude, skin temperature, accelerometer, gyroscope, GPS route capture, and weather alert pairing through the mobile app.

The device stores up to 30 days of activity sessions offline and syncs through Bluetooth when the mobile app reconnects. Each session contains route segments, pace zones, ascent and descent, recovery pauses, ambient temperature, battery events, and safety alert acknowledgements.

The hardware team reports three years of reliable field operation across humid mountains, desert race events, and winter hiking pilots. Firmware reliability is a positive signal for data products because customers already trust the device during real outdoor activities.

The manual does not describe any certified medical measurement mode. The heart-rate sensor is intended for fitness and training guidance only. It is not cleared for diagnosis, treatment, or clinical monitoring.
