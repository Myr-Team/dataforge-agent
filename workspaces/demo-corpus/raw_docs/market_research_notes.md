# Market Research Notes

Comparable products include Strava, TrainingPeaks, Garmin Connect, and Nix Biosensors. They prove demand for training analytics, route comparison, and performance subscriptions, but they also raise the bar for differentiated positioning.

TrailSense can differentiate around rugged outdoor telemetry, group safety context, weather-adjusted route strain, and hardware-specific signal quality. This is strongest for B2B guide operators and endurance coaching teams that need event reports.

Market risk: pure consumer fitness dashboards are crowded. A product should avoid generic "another activity app" positioning and focus on evidence-backed insights from TrailSense's hardware and outdoor telemetry.

Medical diagnosis is not a credible near-term positioning. Competitors that make health claims either avoid diagnosis language or operate with regulated medical validation and clinical partnerships.
