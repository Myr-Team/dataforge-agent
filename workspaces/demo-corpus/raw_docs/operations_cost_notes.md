# Operations Cost Notes

The data team estimates that a first product can run on nightly batch processing, AI Search retrieval over product documents, and a small reporting service. Cost is expected to be manageable for a pilot with 20 B2B customers.

Support cost increases if the product makes individualized medical claims because every false positive needs escalation, review, and potentially regulated complaint handling.

Recommended pilot scope: outdoor performance analytics, group safety preparation reports, and route strain explanations. Avoid health diagnosis until the company funds a separate regulated program.
